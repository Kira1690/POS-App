## 📞 ESCALATION PROCEDURES

### Issue Escalation Levels
1. **Level 1 - Minor Delay** (< 2 hours behind)
   - Self-resolve with overtime or scope adjustment
   - Document in daily progress update

2. **Level 2 - Significant Blocker** (2-4 hours behind)
   - Reassess timeline and priorities
   - Consider parallel work streams
   - Update milestone targets

3. **Level 3 - Critical Risk** (> 4 hours behind or quality issues)
   - Emergency planning session
   - Consider scope reduction
   - Stakeholder notification

### Recovery Strategies
- **Time Recovery**: Parallel development, scope prioritization
- **Quality Recovery**: Extended testing phase, iterative improvements
- **Technical Recovery**: Fallback implementations, simplified approaches
